these plots exist to show that the batch correction worked. They were created from the batch corrected file
meaning they contain all cell types. They are normalized bcs of getnormalizedexpression from scanvi.
they are not logarithmized, but that does not matter because we can still see great seperation in the
UMAPs of cell types and better batch mixture than in the uncorrected UMAP.

Also consider that the PCA is less expressive here than the UMAP because UMAP shows neighborhood closeness
and PCA does not (and we care about this type of cloeseness here because same cell type would be 
close to eachother)
